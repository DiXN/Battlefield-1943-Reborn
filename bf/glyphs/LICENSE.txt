Only the pictures our prompts use are taken from the pack, trimmed and resized by
tools/make_kenney_glyphs.py, with a thin dark rim added so they read on light paper.
keyboard/WASD.png is laid out there from the pack's W, A, S and D keys.

devices/ (the controllers, the keyboard and the mouse in Settings > Controls) are our own
drawings, made by runs/ui-designs/build_devices.py for Battlefield 1943 Reborn. Each controller's
outline and button places come from the controller silhouettes in Kenney's "Input Prompts" (CC0,
https://kenney.nl/assets/input-prompts); no picture from any maker and no maker's logo.
devices/anchors.txt gives each picture's button places.
